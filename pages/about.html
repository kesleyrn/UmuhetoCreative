About us

Fashion built from where we come from.

UMUHETO Creative is a forward-thinking fashion house rooted in Rwanda and shaped for a global audience. “UMUHETO,” meaning bow in Kinyarwanda, symbolises precision, strength, and focus — values that define our approach to design, craft, and culture.

Umuheto Story

UMUHETO Creative was born from a passion for creativity, a love for Rwandan culture, and a vision to transform our heritage into modern fashion.

Founded by Olivier Nyirumuheto, UMUHETO Creative is a Rwandan fashion house that brings together tradition, contemporary design, and timeless elegance. Our journey is rooted in the belief that fashion is more than clothing — it is a powerful expression of identity, pride, and cultural heritage.

Inspired by the richness of Rwandan culture, we reinterpret traditional elements into distinctive designs that connect our roots with the future. Every creation tells a story, celebrates our identity, and reflects the beauty of where we come from.

Our journey has been shaped by passion, perseverance, and the courage to pursue a vision even in the face of challenges. Through creativity and craftsmanship, we continue to explore new ways of expressing our culture while creating meaningful fashion for the modern world.

At UMUHETO Creative, we design for individuals who value originality, elegance, and identity. From everyday looks to special occasions, our creations celebrate the confidence of wearing something that represents who you are and where you come from.

Our vision is to build a trusted Rwandan fashion brand recognized locally and internationally for its creativity, quality, and distinctive cultural expression.

We believe that our culture is an endless source of creative ideas — a heritage to preserve, celebrate, and share with the world.

We don’t just create clothes. We craft pride, identity, and legacy.

UMUHETO Creative — Our culture, the origin of new creative ideas.

Olivier Nyirumuheto Founder & Creative Director of UMUHETO Creative

Story of Nyirumuheto

Olivier Nyirumuheto

Founder & Creative Director of UMUHETO Creative

Drawing, music, and my father’s shoe shop sparked my creativity. At 12, I began shaping my own looks with his shirts, suits, and hats. In 2015, I won the Kinyaga Awards for a second time. Blending Rwandan tradition with modern style in my performances showed me that fashion could express identity and confidence.

From 2016 to 2019, I learned garment-making from the cutting room up at C&H in Masoro and later Supreme. In 2019, I set out as an independent designer, determined to keep creating despite limited resources. Being named among the Top 10 at ART Rwanda Ubuhanzi in 2023 renewed my confidence and opened new doors. I’ve since created looks for Bruce Melody, Alyn Sano, Man Martin, and Masamba Intore.

Today, UMUHETO transforms Rwandan heritage into distinctive, timeless fashion—telling our stories and building a leading Rwandan brand for the world.

Inspiration

Ideas are all around us.

Our inspiration comes first from Rwandan culture, and also from the cultures of other countries, explored with curiosity and respect. Vintage style gives us timeless ideas to reinterpret, while the environment around us—our everyday surroundings, colors, and textures—offers constant inspiration. Our moods and feelings matter too: they help spark ideas and shape what we create.
