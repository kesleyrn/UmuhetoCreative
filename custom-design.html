Custom design

Built for you, from the first sketch.

We specialise in creating custom-made clothing for women and men, using only the finest materials. Here's how a bespoke order comes together.

5 steps consultation to delivery
Made to measure cut precisely for you
By appointment in Remera or remotely

The process

From consultation to delivery

1

Consultation

WhatsApp or studio

Reach us on WhatsApp or phone, or visit the studio in Remera. We talk through the occasion, the reference pieces you like, and your timeline.

2

Design & fabric selection

Heritage detailing

We propose a silhouette and heritage detailing — Umwashi, Umushanana, or a look of your own — and select the fabric together.

3

Measurement

In-studio or remote

In-studio or guided remotely, we take full measurements to ensure the piece is cut precisely for you.

4

Fitting

First fitting

A first fitting lets us adjust fit and finish before the final construction begins.

5

Delivery

Collection or delivery

Your finished piece is ready for collection at the studio or arranged for delivery ahead of your event.

Best for

Occasions that deserve a made-to-measure piece

Traditional weddings and introductions, milestone celebrations, and everyday pieces you want cut exactly to your body and your taste.

Each order is made by hand, with fittings by appointment — nothing leaves the studio until it is cut and finished for you.
