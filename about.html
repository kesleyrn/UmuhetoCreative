About us

Fashion built from where we come from.

Umuheto Creative is a forward-thinking fashion house rooted in Rwanda and shaped for a global audience. “UMUHETO,” meaning bow in Kinyarwanda, symbolises precision, strength, and focus — values that define our approach to design, craft, and culture.

Vision leading African fashion

Our story

Culture as the source of new ideas

We are a house of fashion specialising in custom-made clothing for women and men, using only the finest materials.

Our designs draw on traditional Rwandan & African culture and vintage style, translated into pieces built for how people actually dress today. Every collection begins with a heritage reference — a weave, a silhouette, a ceremony — before it becomes a modern garment.

Our vision is to become a leading African fashion brand that celebrates cultural heritage through innovation, inspiring pride, creativity, and identity while reimagining tradition, Rwanda, and African artistry for the modern world.
