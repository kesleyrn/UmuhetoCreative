What we do

Cultural apparel,
cut for now.

Umuheto Creative designs modern cultural apparel and bespoke ceremonial wear for women and men — blending traditional Rwandan heritage with a contemporary hand.

Every silhouette carries a heritage reference: the sweep of the Umushanana, the structure of Umwashi headwear, the formality of ceremony. Each is studied, reworked, and cut for how people actually dress today.

About the house

A house of fashion, built on Rwandan & African craft

Umuheto Creative specialises in custom-made clothing for women and men using only the finest materials. Our designs are inspired by traditional Rwandan & African culture and vintage style — because everyone deserves to look and feel their best.

The house is led by creative director Talento Nyirumuheto, whose work moves between couture craft, ceremony, and modern street.
