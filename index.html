What we do

Made for ceremony,
for daily life, and everything in between.

UMUHETO Creative is a forward-thinking Rwandan fashion house that blends creativity, technology, and cultural expression. Our vision is to become a leading African fashion brand that reimagines Rwandan and African heritage through modern design — celebrating identity, pride, and creativity worldwide.

Each design begins with the wearer: the occasion, the fit, the fabric, and the feeling. The result is a garment designed to move naturally, hold presence, and feel distinctly personal while staying rooted in culture.

About the house

A house of fashion, built on Rwandan & African craft

UMUHETO Creative specialises in custom-made clothing for women and men using only the finest materials. Our designs are inspired by traditional Rwandan & African culture and vintage style, bringing heritage into contemporary fashion with confidence and purpose.

Our core values are cultural pride, creativity and innovation, and precision rooted in focus. “UMUHETO,” meaning “bow” in Kinyarwanda, symbolises strength, discipline, and the clarity behind every piece we create.
